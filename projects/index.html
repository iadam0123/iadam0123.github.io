What is demonstrated — and what is not

Demonstrated: authorization-aware retrieval logic, deterministic policy enforcement, approval gates, event provenance, explainable risk scoring, usable browser interfaces and exportable artifacts.

Not claimed: production AI model integration, actual enterprise connections, audited compliance, persistent audit immutability, verified high availability, real-world client delivery or live cloud deployment of back-end services.

Next production stages: authenticated APIs, enterprise vector search, LLM service, policy engine, durable audit storage, CI/CD and controlled security testing.
